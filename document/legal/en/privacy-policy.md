## I. Introduction

LucrTrade Limited ("LucrTrade," "we," "us," or "our"), a company incorporated in Hong Kong, operates the website at lucrtrade.com and provides trading education, community, tools, and market intelligence services (collectively, the "Platform").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data when you visit our website, join our Discord community, subscribe to our services, or otherwise interact with the Platform.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection and use of your personal data as described herein.

**If you do not agree to this Privacy Policy, please stop using the Platform immediately.**

## II. Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following categories:

### 1. Account and Registration Data

When you register for an account or subscribe to a LucrTrade membership, we collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Username (if applicable)
- Date of account creation
- Membership tier and subscription status

### 2. Payment Data

When you purchase a Premium Membership or any paid service, payment transactions are processed by **Stripe**, our third-party payment processor. We do not directly collect or store your full card details. Through Stripe's processing, the following information is associated with your transaction:

- Billing name
- Billing email address
- Last four digits of your payment card (stored by Stripe)
- Card type and expiry date (stored by Stripe)
- Transaction amount, date, and status
- Stripe customer ID and transaction ID

For Stripe's own data practices, please refer to Stripe's Privacy Policy at stripe.com/privacy.

### 3. Community and Platform Interaction Data

When you participate in our Discord community or interact with Platform content, we may collect or observe:

- Content you post, share, or comment on in community channels (e.g., trade ideas, analysis, feedback)
- Your activity level and general engagement within the community

**Important:** Our Discord community operates on Discord's platform. Discord independently collects and processes your data according to its own Privacy Policy, available at discord.com/privacy. LucrTrade does not control Discord's data collection or processing.

### 4. TradingView Access Data

To provision your access to our TradingView indicators, we may collect your TradingView username solely for the purpose of granting indicator access permissions on the TradingView platform. This data is not used for any other purpose.

### 5. Communications Data

If you contact us via email or any support channel, we collect:

- Your name and email address
- The content and history of your communication with us

### 6. Website Technical Data

When you visit lucrtrade.com, we automatically collect certain technical information via **Google Analytics** (website analytics) and **Cloudflare** (infrastructure and security), including:

- IP address and approximate location (country/region level only; IP addresses are anonymized before processing by Google Analytics)
- Browser type, version, and operating system
- Referring website and pages visited on lucrtrade.com
- Date and time of access
- Device type and screen resolution
- Session duration and traffic source

See Section VII for details on cookies and tracking technologies in use.

### 7. LucrJournal Data

**LucrJournal is a strictly local-first tool. All data you enter into LucrJournal is stored exclusively on your own device.** LucrTrade does not collect, receive, process, store, or have any access to your LucrJournal data — including your trade records, journal entries, personal notes, or any data processed by AI agents you connect to LucrJournal. You are solely responsible for the security and backup of your local LucrJournal data.

## VII. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following cookies and tracking technologies on lucrtrade.com:

### 1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Platform to function and cannot be disabled.

| Cookie / Technology                                           | Provider         | Purpose                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stripe session and fraud-prevention cookies                   | Stripe           | Required to process payments securely. Set only on pages where payment functionality is active. Governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy at stripe.com/privacy.                                                    |
| Cloudflare security cookies (e.g., `__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance`) | Cloudflare, Inc. | Used by Cloudflare to distinguish human users from automated bots, and to maintain CDN, DDoS protection, and security functionality. Governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. |

### 2. Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve Platform performance and user experience.

| Cookie / Technology                 | Provider   | Purpose                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google Analytics 4 (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) | Google LLC | Tracks anonymized usage data including pages visited, session duration, traffic sources, and approximate geographic location. IP addresses are anonymized before any processing. |

Google Analytics is configured with **IP anonymization enabled**. The data collected is aggregated and does not identify you personally. LucrTrade does not use Google Analytics for advertising or remarketing purposes.

**Opting out of Google Analytics:** You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time by:

- Installing the **Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on**, available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout; or
- Adjusting your browser's cookie settings to block or delete `_ga` and `_ga_*` cookies.

### 3. No Advertising or Tracking Pixels

We do not use any advertising or third-party tracking pixels (such as Facebook Pixel, Google Ads Pixel, or similar) on lucrtrade.com.

### 4. No Cookie Consent Banner

We have assessed our cookie usage and determined that:

- Strictly necessary cookies (Stripe, Cloudflare) are exempt from consent requirements;
- Google Analytics is configured with IP anonymization and collects only aggregated, anonymized data.

Based on this assessment and the primary legal framework applicable to the Platform (Hong Kong PDPO), we do not currently operate a cookie consent management banner (CMP). If you wish to opt out of analytics cookies, please use the opt-out methods described above.

**Managing cookies generally:** You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies (Stripe, Cloudflare) may impair your ability to use certain Platform features, including payment processing.

## XI. International Users and Applicable Law

This Platform is operated by LucrTrade Limited, incorporated in Hong Kong, and is primarily governed by the **Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong ("PDPO")**.

We serve members across multiple regions. While the privacy laws of different jurisdictions differ in their specific requirements, they share common foundational principles — including transparency, purpose limitation, data minimization, security, and individual rights. This Privacy Policy is designed to uphold these principles across all regions we serve.

**Users in Taiwan (台灣用戶)** — Your personal data may additionally be subject to the Personal Information Protection Act (個人資料保護法, "PIPA"). The rights described in Section X are consistent with the data subject rights available to you under Taiwan's PIPA, including your rights of access, correction, deletion, and restriction of use.

**Users in Malaysia** — Your personal data may additionally be subject to the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA"). We process your personal data lawfully and transparently, and only for the purposes stated in this Policy, in a manner consistent with the seven data protection principles under Malaysia's PDPA.

**Users in Singapore** — Your personal data may additionally be subject to the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 ("PDPA"). We collect, use, and disclose personal data only for purposes that a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the circumstances, consistent with Singapore's accountability and purpose limitation obligations.

**Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom** — If you access the Platform from the EEA or UK, you may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Our legal bases for processing are identified in Section III.

Users accessing the Platform from outside Hong Kong do so at their own risk and are solely responsible for ensuring their use of the Platform complies with any additional local laws applicable to them.
