## LucrJournal and your vault

LucrJournal is a local-first Obsidian plugin. Your records live in the current vault rather than only in a remote service.

This means:

- You can search records in Obsidian.
- You can link trades, News, Analysis, and Playbooks.
- You can back up the whole vault.
- You are also responsible for careful direct edits and deletion.

## What Markdown means for you

Markdown keeps records readable, searchable, and linkable. You do not need to learn every Markdown feature to use LucrJournal.

The common pieces are:

- Plain paragraphs for Notes and review conclusions.
- Lists for checklists.
- Wikilinks for connecting related records, such as [[q-and-a]].
- `==highlight==` for marking critical limits or conclusions.

## How to use wikilinks

When you want to connect one document to another, use a wikilink.

Example:

```md
[[q-and-a]]
[[dashboard-review]]
```

If the link points to a LucrJournal bundled doc, it opens in the document view. You can still use Obsidian links in your own vault notes.

## Attachment files

Screenshots and image attachments are part of your review evidence. They should explain trade context, execution, or result.

Before cleaning up attachments, confirm:

- Whether another position still references the image.
- Whether it is the only evidence.
- Whether the important information is already written in Notes.

## Be careful with direct edits

Direct editing is fine for text, Notes, and review conclusions. Be careful with content that affects Accounts, Symbols, position links, and stats.

> [!warning]
> If you are not sure what a change affects, edit from the LucrJournal UI first.
