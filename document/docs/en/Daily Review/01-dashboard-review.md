## What to read first each day

Dashboard is the entry point for reviewing trades. Use this order:

1. Read Overview to understand the broad result.
2. Open Positions to find trades that need review.
3. Check News and Analysis for context.
4. Open Your Playbooks to review setup behavior.
5. Use Settings only when you need configuration.

Do not start by changing settings. Start with records and review.

## Overview

Use Overview to scan the current period. Check Net Profit, Win Rate, timeframe, and broad changes there.

Overview is not the conclusion. When something looks unusual, go to Positions and find the sample.

## Positions

Positions is where you search, filter, and open specific trades.

Common actions:

- Filter by Account.
- Filter by Symbol.
- Search Symbol.
- Review Profit and result.
- Open a position for deeper review.

%% ![[screenshot-positions.png]] %%

> [!tip]
> If you do not know which trade to review, sort by recent activity, then open the largest loss or the position that deviated most from the plan.

## News and Analysis

News, Key Level, Market Analysis, and Confluence explain the trade background. Use them to answer:

- What external event happened around the trade?
- Where was the Key Level?
- Did the same Confluence appear repeatedly?
- Did this background affect multiple positions?

## Your Playbooks

Your Playbooks let you review a setup type instead of only one trade. After steady use, a Playbook should show:

- Which setups you trade repeatedly.
- Which criteria are often met or ignored.
- Which positions are good examples or counterexamples.
