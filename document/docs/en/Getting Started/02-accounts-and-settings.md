## What Accounts solve

Accounts separate your trading sources. They affect position ownership, Symbol ownership, Dashboard filters, and later review.

If you use multiple brokers, exchanges, or demo accounts, do not mix them into one Account. Mixing them makes positions, Symbols, and stats harder to explain.

## Create your first Account

1. Open Journal.
2. Go to Accounts in Settings.
3. Choose or type a Platform.
4. Enter an Account Name.
5. Save the Account.

%% ![[screenshot-settings-accounts.png]] %%

Use a name that future you can recognize. A broker name, account purpose, or funding source is usually clearer than only "main".

## Add a Symbol

A Symbol belongs to an Account. Before creating a position, confirm that the target Account already has the Symbol you want to trade.

1. Go to Symbols in Settings.
2. Choose the Account.
3. Enter the Symbol name.
4. Save it.

%% ![[screenshot-settings-symbols.png]] %%

> [!note]
> Confirm the Account first, then confirm the Symbol. The same Symbol can mean different trading environments under different Accounts.

## Before editing or deleting

Changing an Account name affects how you recognize positions later. Deleting an Account or Symbol affects linked positions.

> [!warning]
> Before deleting an Account or Symbol, confirm whether the linked positions still need to be kept. Delete is best for cleaning up mistaken data, not for organizing long-term history.

## When to return here

- You add a broker or exchange.
- You separate demo and real accounts.
- You prepare different Accounts for different strategies.
- You find a position under the wrong Account.
